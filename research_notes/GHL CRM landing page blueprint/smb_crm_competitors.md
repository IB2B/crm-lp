# SMB CRM and All-in-One Competitor Landing and Pricing Page Teardown

All pages were fetched live on **2026-10-08**, and every price is as observed on that date. The fetch tool returns extracted text, not rendered pages, so visual details are inferred from alt text and labels: hero images, badge placement and highlighted-plan styling. Pipedrive's pricing page returned HTTP 403, so Pipedrive pricing is not covered (see Gaps).

## 1. Per-competitor teardown: hero, CTAs, section order, features, media, proof, pricing, FAQ, migration, trust

### Takeaway
Two templates dominate. Sales CRMs (HubSpot, Pipedrive, Close, Zoho, Bigin, Monday) use **free plan or 14–15-day no-card trial + per-seat tiers + G2 proof + AI agent spotlight**. Creator and agency all-in-ones (Kartra, systeme.io, Keap, Vendasta, HighLevel) use **flat-price tiers + "replaces X tools" + outcome stories**. Keap and Vendasta are the only ones that make onboarding or implementation a visible, paid part of the offer.

## 3. Gaps: what no competitor claims that an all-in-one, done-for-you, agency-backed CRM could

### Takeaway
No competitor combines **(a) a full omnichannel inbox (SMS, email, WhatsApp, social DMs), (b) funnels, booking, reviews and payments in one product, (c) done-for-you setup included in the price, and (d) a named human team or agency accountable for results**. Keap gets closest on done-for-you but charges for it, sells a single plan at $299+/mo and positions as automation software. Vendasta targets agencies, not end SMBs.

### Cited Findings
- Sales CRMs (Pipedrive, Close, HubSpot Starter, Bigin, Monday) sell **pipeline and sales activity**. None of their heroes mention websites or funnels, reputation and reviews, or booking calendars as core. — [Pipedrive](https://www.pipedrive.com/en); [Close](https://www.close.com/); [Bigin](https://www.bigin.com/pricing.html)
- Done-for-you setup is either **paid or required** or limited to top tiers:
  - Keap requires an unpriced implementation — [Keap](https://keap.com/pricing)
  - Vendasta charges an at-signing fee plus "Setup as a Service" — [Vendasta](https://www.vendasta.com/pricing/)
  - Monday offers white-glove migration only on Ultimate — [monday](https://monday.com/crm/pricing)
  - Zoho sells Jumpstart as a paid service and includes migration only in Ultimate — [Zoho](https://www.zoho.com/crm/zohocrm-pricing.html)
- Self-serve "free migration" exists but is importer-led (Close importers, Bigin one-click) or tier-gated (systeme.io Unlimited). — [Close](https://www.close.com/pricing); [Bigin](https://www.bigin.com/pricing.html); [systeme.io](https://systeme.io/pricing)
- Per-seat pricing with seat minimums is the norm for sales CRMs: Monday requires 3 seats; HubSpot, Zoho and Close charge per seat. — [monday](https://monday.com/crm/pricing); [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/crm)
- HighLevel itself discloses telecom and AI usage as separate charges. Keap charges for SMS tiers and extra users. Close bills calling per minute. — [HighLevel](https://www.gohighlevel.com/pricing); [Keap](https://keap.com/pricing); [Close](https://www.close.com/pricing)
- Close already offers a **HighLevel importer**, meaning it targets GHL users, and HighLevel publishes vs-pages against HubSpot, Keap, Kartra, Vendasta, Zoho, ActiveCampaign and others. — [Close pricing](https://www.close.com/pricing); [HighLevel](https://www.gohighlevel.com/pricing)

### Inferences (claims available to an agency-backed white-label GHL CRM)
1. **"Done-for-you setup included."** Offer pipelines, automations, a website or funnel and calendars built for the customer within X days. It answers Keap's required paid implementation and Vendasta's at-signing fee.
2. **"A real team, not a ticket queue."** Offer a named onboarding specialist or agency, local language and time zone. No mainstream CRM sells accountability for outcomes.
3. **"Every conversation in one inbox."** Combine SMS, email, WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook DMs, Google Business chat and reviews. Competitors list channels in tables but none lead with it in the hero.
4. **"Replace your stack" with an explicit calculator.** Use local SMB tools, not creator tools: CRM + Calendly + Mailchimp + website builder + review tool + chatbot + invoicing versus one price. Only systeme.io does stack math, and it targets creators.
5. **Flat price, unlimited users.** Contrast this with per-seat pricing and seat minimums at HubSpot, Zoho, Close and Monday.
6. **Industry snapshots.** Offer prebuilt setups for clinics, real estate, home services and similar verticals. Competitors group features by module or generic outcome, not by vertical.
7. **AI agents that work on day one, configured for you.** Competitors sell AI agents but expect self-configuration. Vendasta comes closest, but only through agencies.
8. **Transparent usage pricing.** Publish SMS, call and AI usage rates up front to address the "hidden fees" FAQ that appears at HighLevel and ActiveCampaign.
- Caveat: these are positioning hypotheses from absence of evidence on the pages fetched. They were not validated with customers.

### Gaps
- Competitor vs-pages weren't fetched individually (for example HubSpot vs Pipedrive, Keap vs HubSpot), so their attack angles aren't documented beyond Keap's "#1 HubSpot Alternative" nav claim.
- The regional angle wasn't researched: MENA/EU language, local payment and WhatsApp-first messaging.

### Inferences
- The two-CTA pattern of a self-serve trial or free plan plus "Talk to sales / Get a demo" is near-universal. For a done-for-you offer, a **"Book a free setup call"** primary CTA paired with a "See it in action" video or tour secondary would stand out from the trial-first crowd while matching the service-led model, as Keap and Vendasta do with demo-first.
- Section order converges on: hero → logos or counts → features (with AI as its own block) → integrations → testimonials or metrics → onboarding/support → pricing teaser → FAQ → final CTA. Common FAQ topics: free limits, contracts and cancellation, hidden or usage fees, migration, security and support.

### Gaps
- None of the extracts showed uptime figures. Uptime SLA appears only as an ActiveCampaign tier feature, and Kartra links a status page.
- Interactive product tours (Navattic or Storylane style) weren't observed on any fetched page. That's either a genuine gap or an artifact of the extraction method.
